Method, and what this is not

Records enter polislop only with at least two independent, reputable sources, listed under each entry. The deception score is an editorial assessment against the rubric above — not a legal finding, and not a claim that any ad broke a law. Entries marked provisional have a rubric input that reporting has not yet settled; their scores may move.

The score grades how an ad depicts real people. It does not grade whether the ad's underlying political claims are true — that is fact-checking, and a separate job.

On embedded video

The page frames video from YouTube wherever a record carries a confirmed video ID, with a direct link beneath it so the ad is reachable even if the player is blocked. No record carries one yet: confirming that a given video is the ad in question means watching it against the cited reporting, and an unverified ID would put the wrong footage under a named person's name — the exact error this ledger exists to document. Adding one is a one-line change per record.

Jurisdiction

FCC jurisdiction covers broadcast, cable, satellite and radio. Ads that ran only on social platforms or web video fall outside it, and the ledger says so per record rather than sending you to the wrong agency.
